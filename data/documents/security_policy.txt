Company Security Policy

All employees must protect company, customer, and confidential information.

Access Control:
- Employees must use unique accounts and strong passwords.
- Multi-factor authentication is required for systems containing sensitive information.
- Access should be limited to the minimum required for an employee's role.
- Shared accounts are not permitted.

Data Protection:
- Confidential data must be stored only in approved company systems.
- Sensitive information must not be sent through personal email or unauthorized messaging tools.
- Lost devices or suspected data exposure must be reported immediately.

Security Incidents:
- Employees should report suspected phishing, unauthorized access, or data loss to the security team.
- Security incidents should be reported as soon as they are identified.
- Employees must not investigate suspected incidents independently if doing so could destroy evidence.

Remote Work:
- Company devices must use approved security controls when employees work remotely.
- Public or unsecured networks should not be used for sensitive business activity without approved protections.
