Customer Complaint Handling

Customer complaints should be handled consistently, respectfully, and promptly.

Process:
1. Record the complaint in the customer support system.
2. Confirm receipt with the customer.
3. Classify the complaint by product, severity, and urgency.
4. Assign an owner responsible for follow-up.
5. Investigate the issue using available customer and transaction information.
6. Provide the customer with an update and expected resolution timeline.
7. Escalate complaints involving security, privacy, safety, or regulatory concerns.
8. Record the resolution and close the complaint only after follow-up is complete.
9. Review recurring complaints for trends and potential process improvements.

Service Expectations:
- High-severity complaints receive priority handling.
- Customer communications should be clear and factual.
- Employees should not promise refunds, credits, or other remedies without the required approval.
